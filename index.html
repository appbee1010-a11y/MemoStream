Privacy Policy

Kitty Eye Gym: Eye Exercises

Last Updated: September 2026

🛡️

Zero Registration Required: We never require accounts, logins, or cloud synchronization. Your workout and vision routine remain entirely private on your device.

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Kitty Eye Gym: Eye Exercises ("we", "our", or "us") provides a 3-minute daily wellness exercise routine to refresh tired eyes with interactive feline tracking guides. We strongly believe personal wellness routines should stay personal. The application is built with a strictly client-side architecture that functions completely offline without requiring accounts, passwords, or personal identity verification.

2. Data Collection & Information Handling

We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Account Required: No registration, email login, or identity profiles are ever created.
  • No Camera or Biometric Tracking: The exercises rely solely on manual on-screen gaze tracking. We do not access your camera, face recognition APIs, or eye-tracking biometrics.
  • Local Preference Storage: Settings such as selected trajectory orbits, speed preferences, haptic feedback toggles, and reminder timers are stored locally on your device storage (via SharedPreferences / local key-value store) and never leave your hardware.

3. Device Permissions

The app only requests standard platform capabilities strictly necessary for its functionality:

  • Notifications / Reminders: Used exclusively to deliver daily eye-break schedule reminders if you explicitly opt-in through Settings.
  • Vibration / Haptics: Provides gentle physical feedback during trajectory milestones and workout switches.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

To maintain basic app reliability and performance across thousands of device models, we may use standard, privacy-preserving mobile development toolkits (such as Apple StoreKit or Google Play Core Services) for handling in-app purchases or localized performance metrics. These tools do not associate your usage with personal profiles.

5. Medical & Wellness Disclaimer

Kitty Eye Gym is an eye relaxation, focus stretching, and wellness utility designed to reduce ocular fatigue from digital screens. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any ocular pathology, optical disease, or clinical refractive condition. Consult a qualified ophthalmologist or optometrist for clinical eye care.

6. Children’s Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Because we do not gather personal identifiers or track locations, our application complies inherently with the requirements of COPPA and child data protection frameworks. We encourage users of all ages to practice healthy digital screen habits.

7. Policy Modifications

We may occasionally update this statement to reflect updates to app store regulations or system architecture. Changes take effect upon posting to this page.
