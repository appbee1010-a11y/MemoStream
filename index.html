Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how "MemoStream" (hereinafter referred to as "the Application") collects, uses, and manages your information.

1. Information We Collect

To provide chat-style note-taking and cloud synchronization features, the Application collects and utilizes the following user-provided or authorized information:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and profile photo URL (when using account sign-in features).
  • Memo Data: Chat room details, notes, text messages, URLs of attached images and files, and link preview metadata created by the user.
  • Application Settings: Language preferences, theme modes, and tag visibility configurations.

This information is used solely for the purpose of securely recording, managing, and synchronizing your memos within the Application.

2. Data Transmission and Third-Party Sharing

To support real-time multi-device synchronization and backup, the Application securely transmits and stores data using Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore). We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal user information with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

3. Advertisements and Analytics

The Application does not include third-party advertising SDKs for behavioral targeting, nor does it employ analytics SDKs designed to track or identify individual users.

4. Device Permissions and Access

When attaching photos or documents to your memos, the Application utilizes standard system pickers (such as Android Storage Access Framework / Photo Picker). The Application does not request or require broad or unnecessary access to your device's external storage.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your notes and configurations are stored both in your device's local database and securely in the cloud (when signed in).

Users can permanently delete their account and all associated cloud and local data at any time via the Settings menu within the Application. Please note that simply uninstalling the Application does not automatically erase your synchronized cloud data; if you wish to delete all data completely, please perform the account deletion procedure inside the Application.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in response to evolving legal, technical, or operational developments. Any significant changes will be communicated via the Application or posted on this page.
